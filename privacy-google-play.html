Meow on the Beat — Privacy Policy

Updated: November 11, 2026
Applies to: Google Play Android release 0.10.14 (version code 114), with optional AdMob rewarded ads
Package name: com.leo.pawbeats
Developer and operator: Lei Shaojun (雷少俊), an individual developer
Developer display name: Tob工作室
Privacy contact: 869167056@qq.com

Meow on the Beat (喵喵踩点) is a cat rhythm game. “We” means the developer identified above. This policy covers this advertising release. The earlier ad-free 0.10.13-test / 113 release does not gain advertising or new data processing merely because this policy is updated. Its policy is at https://lei123666.github.io/privacy-google-play-v0.10.13.html.

1. Local play and progress

Your taps, timing and level results are processed on your device. Progress, unlocked levels, scores, ratings, language, volume, control hints and rhythm calibration are saved in the app’s local storage. Rewarded-ad unlocks are also saved locally. The game does not upload these saved records to the developer. There is no game account, payment, chat or user-content upload feature. The developer does not add a separate analytics or crash-reporting SDK; the advertising SDK has its own measurement and diagnostics described below.

Clearing app data or uninstalling may permanently remove progress and ad rewards. We do not keep a server copy to restore them. Device or platform backup behaviour depends on that service and your settings.

2. Optional rewarded ads

After completing levels 5, 8 and 12, you can choose a clearly labelled rewarded ad to unlock levels 6, 9 and 13 respectively. An ad plays only after you choose to watch. There are no banner, app-open or automatic interstitial ads. You can decline or leave the offer and continue playing already unlocked levels. Existing unlocks are preserved.

An unlock is saved only when the advertising SDK confirms an earned reward. Closing an ad, a load failure, no available ad or a connection error does not itself earn a reward. A valid reward confirmation may arrive shortly after the ad closes. An unlock already saved in the same local save does not require watching again.

3. Google advertising and privacy choices

This release integrates Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). Starting the game does not start these services. Your first choice to watch an ad in each app session starts UMP’s privacy-status check after the age screen described below. This check can contact Google before a message is shown. Applicable messages depend on region, age treatment and the privacy messages configured for this app. The game waits until UMP permits ad requests before initializing advertising or loading an ad. No ad loads simply because you finish a level. Where UMP requires a privacy-options entry, you can reopen it in the game’s settings.

AdMob processes IP-based approximate location, ad interactions and diagnostic information for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Google’s general SDK disclosure also covers device and account identifiers. This release removes Android’s AD_ID permission, applies child age treatment to younger players, requests G-rated ad content and disables personalized advertising for everyone. These limits do not mean the SDK processes no data or that an ad click is anonymous. Google states its SDK data uses TLS in transit. See https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites for Google’s handling, retention and choices.

The game does not provide your local scores, saved progress, name or email address to the advertising SDK, and does not request precise location, contacts, microphone, camera or phone access. It uses network access for these advertising and privacy services, and Android vibration for game feedback. An ad click may open an advertiser’s website or app, which follows its own policy. There are no additional mediation-network SDKs in this release.

4. Children and families

The game is designed for players aged six and older, including children, teenagers and adults. Before the first ad request, a neutral screen asks for a birth year without suggesting an answer. You can skip it and continue with unlocked content without enabling ads. The year is used only on the device to decide age treatment, then discarded; only whether this check was completed and whether restricted treatment applies are saved locally. This version conservatively treats a year-based age below 19 as a child ad request; it does not send your birth year to the developer or Google. Unknown age does not enable advertising.

This screen and Google’s privacy messages do not verify identity or a parent’s consent. No personalized ads are requested for any player. Age treatment and filtering are safeguards, not a promise that the SDK never processes information. Children should ask a parent or guardian before contacting the developer or following an external link. Parents can contact us about information a child has sent to us.

5. Optional email and online services

If you choose to email us, we receive your address, message and any attachments you send, such as screenshots or device details. We use them to reply, investigate an issue or handle a privacy request. The game does not send a support email automatically. Send only necessary information and remove unrelated personal details; never send passwords or verification codes. We do not sell feedback or use it for unrelated advertising. We share nonpublic feedback only as necessary to handle your authorized request or meet a legal requirement.

Google Play, your operating system and email provider separately handle downloads, updates, platform diagnostics and messages under their own settings and policies. The policy website is a static GitHub Pages page without advertising, analytics scripts, external fonts, embedded media or tracking cookies set by us. GitHub may process IP addresses and hosting/security logs: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement. The full in-game policy is available offline.

6. Retention and protection

Local data remains until you delete it or uninstall. Feedback is retained in the receiving mailbox and necessary issue records only while needed to handle the request and applicable legal obligations; we then delete it or remove identifying details. We restrict access and take reasonable protective measures. Google, email and hosting services manage their own retention, security and deletion processes; deleting the game’s local data does not delete information they already hold. We do not promise a single retention period or complete deletion of third-party records.

7. Your choices and privacy requests

You can replay unlocked content without choosing an ad, change game settings and use the advertising privacy-options entry when available. UMP choices affect future requests as supported by Google; changing a choice does not erase earlier processing or your saved rewards. To remove local records, use Android’s “Clear storage” or “Clear data” for the app, or uninstall. There is no game account to close. Android and Google provide their own advertising and privacy controls.

For access, correction, deletion or a privacy concern about information sent to the developer, email 869167056@qq.com with the game name and enough detail to identify the request. A parent or guardian may contact us for a child. Do not send passwords or verification codes. We will respond within applicable legal time limits and explain any limitation. For Google-held data, use Google’s privacy controls or contact Google; we can assist with requests relating to this game but cannot directly delete Google’s records.

8. Policy changes

The date and version identify this policy’s scope. If SDKs, advertising, audience handling or data practices change, we will update the policy and store disclosures before enabling the change and obtain any required consent. Previously playing the ad-free release does not count as consent to advertising. A new policy does not retroactively change the behaviour of an installed older version.

《喵喵踩点》隐私政策

更新日期：2026年11月11日

《喵喵踩点》（Meow on the Beat）是一款猫咪节奏游戏。“我们”指上方列明的开发者。本政策适用于上述广告版本。此前无广告版 0.10.13-test／113 不会因政策更新而自动新增广告或信息处理，其政策见 https://lei123666.github.io/privacy-google-play-v0.10.13.html。

1. 本机游玩与进度

点击、节奏判定和关卡结果在您的设备上处理。进度、已解锁关卡、分数、评价、语言、音量、操作提示和节奏校准保存在应用本机存储空间；广告奖励解锁也在本机保存。游戏不将这些存档记录上传给开发者。游戏没有独立账号、支付、聊天或用户内容上传功能。开发者没有另行接入运营分析或崩溃上报 SDK；广告 SDK 自身的衡量和诊断处理见下文。

清除应用数据或卸载可能永久删除进度和广告奖励。我们没有可供恢复的服务器副本；设备或平台的备份行为取决于对应服务及您的设置。

2. 自愿观看的激励广告

完成第5、8、12关后，您可主动选择标明奖励的广告，分别解锁第6、9、13关。只有您选择观看后才会播放。游戏没有横幅、开屏或自动插屏广告。您可拒绝或退出广告入口，继续游玩已解锁关卡；既有解锁保留。

只有广告 SDK 确认已获得奖励后，游戏才保存解锁。关闭广告、加载失败、无可用广告或网络错误本身不产生奖励；有效奖励确认可能在广告关闭后短时间内到达。同一存档中已保存的解锁无需重复观看。

3. Google 广告与隐私选择

本版本接入 Google Mobile Ads SDK（AdMob）与 Google User Messaging Platform（UMP）。启动游戏不会启动这些服务。每次运行游戏时，您首次选择观看广告，且完成下文的年龄判断后，才会启动 UMP 隐私状态检查；该检查可能在消息出现前连接 Google。实际消息取决于地区、年龄处理及本应用配置的隐私消息。只有 UMP 允许广告请求后，游戏才初始化广告服务或加载广告，不因通关而自动加载。如 UMP 要求提供隐私选项入口，您可在游戏设置中再次打开。

AdMob 为投放、效果衡量和防作弊处理通过 IP 推断的大致位置、广告交互和诊断信息。Google 的通用 SDK 披露也包括设备与账号标识符。本版本移除 Android 的 AD_ID 权限，对低龄玩家采用儿童年龄处理，为所有用户请求 G 级广告内容并关闭个性化广告。这些限制不表示 SDK 完全不处理信息，也不表示点击广告是匿名的。Google 说明其 SDK 数据通过 TLS 加密传输。Google 的信息处理、保存与管理方式见 https://policies.google.com/privacy 和 https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites。

游戏不向广告 SDK 提供您的本机分数、存档、姓名或邮箱，也不申请精确定位、联系人、麦克风、相机或电话访问权限。游戏使用网络访问上述广告和隐私服务，使用 Android 震动提供操作反馈。点击广告可能打开广告主的网站或应用，其信息处理遵循自身政策。本版本没有额外接入聚合广告网络 SDK。

4. 儿童与家庭

本游戏面向6岁及以上玩家，包括儿童、青少年和成年人。首次请求广告前，游戏通过不预设答案的中立页面询问出生年份。您可跳过并继续游玩已解锁内容，不启用广告。年份仅在本机用于判断广告年龄处理，随后丢弃；本机仅保存是否完成判断及是否适用限制处理。本版本采用保守规则，将按年份计算未满19岁的玩家作为儿童广告请求处理，不将出生年份发送给开发者或 Google。年龄未知时不启用广告。

该页面及 Google 隐私消息不核验真实身份或监护人同意。所有用户均不请求个性化广告。年龄处理与内容过滤是保护措施，不代表 SDK 从不处理信息。儿童联系开发者或打开外部链接前，请先征求家长或监护人帮助。监护人可就儿童发送给我们的信息提出请求。

5. 主动邮件与在线服务

如您选择发送邮件，我们会收到您的地址、正文及主动附上的截图或设备详情等材料，用于回复、排查问题或处理隐私请求。游戏不会自动发送支持邮件。请仅发送必要信息，遮挡无关个人内容，不要发送密码或验证码。我们不出售反馈，也不将其用于无关广告。仅在处理经您授权的请求所必需或法律要求时，才另行提供非公开反馈。

Google Play、设备操作系统和邮件服务方按自身规则及设置处理下载、更新、平台诊断和邮件。政策网页由 GitHub Pages 静态托管，不含广告、访问分析脚本、外部字体、嵌入媒体或由我们设置的跟踪 Cookie。GitHub 可能处理 IP 地址及托管、安全日志，详情见 https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement。游戏内可离线阅读完整政策。

6. 保存与保护

本机数据保留至您清除或卸载。反馈仅在处理请求及履行适用法律义务所需期间保存在邮箱和必要的问题记录中；不再需要时删除或去除身份信息。我们限制访问并采取合理保护措施。Google、邮件和托管服务各自管理保存、安全及删除流程；清除游戏本机数据不会删除其已持有的信息。我们不为第三方记录承诺统一保存期限或全部删除。

7. 您的选择与隐私请求

您可不观看广告而重玩已解锁内容，修改游戏设置，并在提供时使用广告隐私选项。UMP 选择按 Google 支持的方式影响后续请求；修改选择不会撤销此前处理或删除已保存奖励。如需删除本机记录，可在 Android 中对本应用选择“清除存储空间”“清除数据”或卸载。游戏没有需要注销的独立账号；Android 和 Google 提供其自身的广告与隐私控制。

如需查阅、更正、删除您发送给开发者的信息，或有隐私疑问，请联系 869167056@qq.com，提供游戏名称和识别请求所需的信息。监护人可代儿童联系。不要提供密码或验证码。我们会在适用法律规定期限内回复，并说明无法满足的部分。Google 持有的数据请通过其隐私控制或联系渠道管理；我们可协助处理与本游戏有关的请求，但不能直接删除 Google 的记录。

8. 政策变更

页首日期与版本说明政策范围。如 SDK、广告、年龄处理或信息处理方式改变，我们会在启用前更新政策及商店声明，并取得所需同意。此前游玩无广告版不代表同意广告；新政策也不追溯改变已安装旧版的实际行为。
