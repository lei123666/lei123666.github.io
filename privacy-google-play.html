Meow on the Beat — Privacy Policy

Updated: October 21, 2026
Applies to: the ad-free Google Play Android test release 0.10.13-test (version code 113)
Package name: com.leo.pawbeats
Developer and operator: Lei Shaojun (雷少俊), an individual developer
Developer display name: Tob工作室
Privacy contact: 869167056@qq.com

Meow on the Beat (喵喵踩点) is a cat rhythm game. This policy explains how information is handled in the ad-free test release identified above. In this policy, “we” means the developer named above. This policy does not describe older advertising test releases or any future release that adds advertising.

1. Information used to play

The game processes your taps, button inputs and timing on your device to run each level and calculate results. It saves level progress, unlocked levels, high scores, best ratings and preferences, such as language, volume, control hints and rhythm calibration, in the app’s local storage. The game does not send these records to us or to third parties.

This release has no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDK. It does not collect an advertising ID, require an account, offer payments or chat, or provide a feature for uploading user content. Playing the game does not require you to give us your name, email address, phone number or other identifying information.

2. Device features

The game uses Android’s vibration feature, where supported, for input feedback. This feature does not give us access to personal information. Local progress and settings are saved in the app’s own storage.

3. Optional email feedback

If you choose to contact us by email, we receive your email address and the message and attachments you choose to send. These may include a description of a problem, screenshots or device details. We use this information to answer you, investigate the issue or handle your privacy request. The game does not automatically send us this information.

Please send only what is necessary and remove unrelated personal information from screenshots. Do not send passwords or verification codes. We do not use feedback for unrelated advertising or sell it. Our email service processes messages as part of delivering and storing mail under its own rules. We do not otherwise share nonpublic feedback unless needed to handle your request with your authorization, or required by law.

4. Children and families

The intended audience includes children aged six and older, as well as teenagers and adults. This ad-free release does not collect children’s personal information through gameplay, and children do not need to create an account or submit personal information to play.

A child who needs help should ask a parent or guardian to contact us. If a child has sent personal information by email, a parent or guardian may ask us to review, correct or delete it using the contact address below. We will use only the information reasonably needed to handle the request. The game has no separate age or parent-identity verification feature.

5. Google Play and the policy website

Google Play and your device’s operating system may separately process information when you download or update the game, use parental controls or enable platform diagnostics. These services follow their own settings and privacy policies. Their processing is separate from the game’s local progress storage. Google’s Privacy Policy is available at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Opening the online policy page uses your browser. The page contains no advertising, analytics scripts, tracking cookies set by us, external fonts or embedded third-party media. The page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process IP addresses and request or security logs to provide and protect its hosting service. That handling is separate from gameplay; see GitHub’s Privacy Statement at https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement. Reading the policy inside the game does not require opening the website.

6. Retention and security

Local progress and settings remain on your device until you clear the app’s data or uninstall it. We do not hold a server copy to restore them. Any backup or restoration offered by your device or platform depends on that service and your settings.

Email feedback is kept in the receiving mailbox and any necessary issue records only for as long as needed to answer or resolve the request and meet applicable legal obligations. When it is no longer needed, we delete it or remove identifying details. We limit our use of feedback to these purposes and take reasonable measures to protect it. Email, platform and hosting providers manage their own storage and security under their policies.

7. Your choices and requests

You can view progress and change preferences in the game. To delete locally stored progress and settings, use “Clear storage” or “Clear data” in Android’s settings for this app, or uninstall the game. This may permanently remove your progress. There is no game account to close. A Google or other platform account is managed through that platform.

To request access to, correction of or deletion of information you have sent us, or to raise a privacy concern, email 869167056@qq.com. Please give the game name and enough details to identify your request. Do not include passwords or verification codes. We will respond within applicable legal time limits and explain if we cannot fulfill a request.

8. Changes to this policy

If a later release adds advertising or other online data processing, we will update the policy and the relevant store disclosures before enabling that functionality, and obtain any consent required for the new processing. Using this ad-free version does not count as consent to future advertising or data collection. The date and applicable version at the top identify the scope of this policy.

《喵喵踩点》隐私政策

《喵喵踩点》（Meow on the Beat）是一款猫咪节奏游戏。本政策说明上述无广告测试版本中的信息处理情况，“我们”指上方列明的开发者。本政策不适用于此前的广告测试版本，也不代表今后新增广告版本的信息处理方式。

1. 游玩时使用的信息

游戏在您的设备上处理点击、按键和节奏判定，用于运行关卡和计算结果。关卡进度、已解锁关卡、最高分、最佳评价以及语言、音量、操作提示、节奏校准等设置保存在应用的本机存储空间。游戏不将这些记录发送给我们或第三方。

本版本没有接入广告、运营统计或崩溃上报 SDK，不收集广告 ID，没有账号注册、支付、聊天或用户内容上传功能。游玩无需向我们提供姓名、邮箱、电话号码等身份信息。

2. 设备功能

设备支持时，游戏使用 Android 震动功能提供操作反馈，该功能不会让我们获取个人信息。游戏进度和设置保存在应用自身的存储空间。

3. 您主动发送的邮件反馈

如果您选择通过邮件联系开发者，我们会收到您的邮件地址以及您主动发送的正文和附件，其中可能包括问题描述、截图或设备信息。我们仅将这些信息用于回复、排查问题或处理隐私请求，游戏不会自动向我们发送这些材料。

请仅发送必要信息，并遮挡截图中无关的个人信息，不要发送密码或验证码。我们不将反馈用于无关广告营销，也不出售反馈信息。邮件服务方会按其规则处理邮件的传送和保存。除经您授权为处理请求所必需，或法律要求的情形外，我们不会另行提供您的非公开反馈。

4. 儿童与家庭

本游戏的目标玩家包括6岁及以上儿童、青少年和成年人。本无广告版本不通过游戏过程收集儿童个人信息，儿童无需创建账号或提交个人信息即可游玩。

儿童如需帮助，请由家长或监护人联系开发者。如果儿童已经通过邮件提交个人信息，家长或监护人可使用下方邮箱申请查阅、更正或删除。我们仅使用处理请求所合理必要的信息。游戏没有独立的年龄识别或监护人身份核验功能。

5. Google Play 与政策网页

通过 Google Play 下载、更新游戏，使用家长控制或启用平台诊断时，Google Play 和设备操作系统可能另行处理信息，相关处理遵循其自身设置和隐私规则，与游戏在本机保存进度的行为不同。Google 隐私政策：https://policies.google.com/privacy。

打开在线政策页面时，您会使用浏览器。该页面不含广告、访问分析脚本、由我们设置的跟踪 Cookie、外部字体或嵌入的第三方媒体。网页由 GitHub Pages 托管，GitHub 可能为提供和保护托管服务处理 IP 地址、请求或安全日志。这与游戏过程中的信息处理分开，详情见 GitHub 隐私声明：https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement。在游戏内阅读本政策不需要打开网页。

6. 保存期限与信息安全

本机进度和设置保留至您清除应用数据或卸载游戏。我们没有可供恢复的服务器副本。设备或平台是否提供备份与恢复功能，取决于相应服务及您的设置。

邮件反馈保存在接收邮件的邮箱和必要的问题处理记录中，仅在回复、解决请求及履行适用法律义务所需期间保留。不再需要时，我们会删除相关信息或去除可识别个人的内容。我们限制反馈的使用范围，并采取合理措施保护信息。邮件、平台与托管服务方按其自身规则管理存储和安全。

7. 您的选择与请求

您可在游戏内查看进度、修改设置。需要删除本机进度和设置时，可在 Android 的应用设置中选择“清除存储空间”或“清除数据”，也可卸载游戏。这可能永久删除您的进度。游戏没有需要注销的独立账号；Google 等平台账号请通过对应平台管理。

如需查阅、更正或删除您主动发送给我们的信息，或有隐私疑问，请联系 869167056@qq.com，并提供游戏名称和识别请求所需的信息，不要提供密码或验证码。我们会在适用法律规定的期限内回复；如无法满足请求，会说明原因。

8. 政策变更

后续版本如新增广告或其他联网信息处理，我们会在启用相应功能前更新政策及商店声明，并为新增处理取得必要的同意。使用本无广告版本不代表您同意未来的广告或信息收集。页首日期和适用版本用于说明本政策的范围。
